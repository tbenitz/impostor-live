How to play

1. Join. Host opens this page on the TV (or laptop). Everyone scans the QR and chooses a name. The host needs at least three connected players.

2. Secret roles. Phones privately reveal Crewmate or Impostor when the round begins. The big TV never reveals roles while playing.

3. Explore. Move with your thumb joystick, tap a destination, or use WASD/arrow keys. Crew approach glowing stations, tap TASK, and complete a 3-step console. Dead crew can still finish tasks as ghosts.

4. Impostor. Get close enough to use KILL after cooldown. Use SABOTAGE to dim crew vision. Don't get caught.

5. Meetings. Report a body or use one emergency meeting. Alive players discuss, then vote privately on their phones. A tie or skip means nobody is ejected. Dead players and spectators cannot vote.

6. Win. Crew win by completing all tasks or ejecting every impostor. Impostors win when they equal/outnumber living crew, or when the round timer expires.

7. Late joiners. Anyone who joins during play watches as a spectator until the next round. Host keeps this tab open because it is the authoritative game device.

Connection note: The single HTML works when hosted on HTTPS (such as GitHub Pages). PeerJS Cloud handles connection setup, then WebRTC carries the game data. Some strict networks may block peer connections.
